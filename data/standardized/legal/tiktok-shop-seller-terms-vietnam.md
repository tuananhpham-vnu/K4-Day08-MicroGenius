1. Gioi Thieu
Cac ieu Khoan Dich Vu nay (bao gom cac Phu Luc, Phu Chu va Phu Chuong) (goi chung cung
nhau la  ieu Khoan ) ieu chinh viec su dung TikTok Shop e quang ba va ban ( Chao Ban )
hang hoa ( San Pham ) boi cac thuong nhan ( Nguoi Ban/Ban ) cho nguoi dung cua ung dung
TikTok ( Nen Tang ) ( Nguoi Mua/Nguoi Dung ), cung nhu viec Ban su dung Thi Truong Dich
Vu va ang ky Dich Vu Cua oi Tac uoc cung cap boi oi Tac, va su dung Dich Vu Kho Tich Hop
uoc cung cap boi Kho Tich Hop (theo cach cac thuat ngu nhu vay uoc inh nghia trong ieu

Khoan nay).
Chu the cung cap Nen Tang cho Ban la chu the cung cap TikTok Shop cho Ban. Ban co the
tim hieu chi tiet ve chu the tuong ung cung cap Nen Tang tai ieu Khoan Dich Vu Cua
TikTok . ieu nay phu thuoc vao noi thuong tru cua Ban. Trong cac ieu Khoan nay, (cac)
chu the ma Ban giao ket hop ong ( TikTok/Chung Toi ) tuy thuoc vao noi thuong tru cua
Ban. Khi cac ieu Khoan nay e cap en mot Ben Lien Ket Cua TikTok, thi thuat ngu nay co
nghia la bat ky chu the nao truc tiep hoac gian tiep kiem soat, uoc kiem soat boi, hoac
chiu su kiem soat chung voi Chung Toi.
2. Chap Thuan Cac ieu Khoan Nay
Bang viec nhap chon nut ong Y hoac truy cap hoac su dung TikTok Shop, Ban xac nhan rang
Ban co the ky ket mot hop ong mang tinh rang buoc voi TikTok, rang Ban chap thuan cac
ieu Khoan nay va rang Ban ong y tuan thu cac ieu Khoan nay.
Viec ban truy cap va su dung dich vu cua Chung Toi cung phai tuan thu theo ieu Khoan
Dich Vu Cua TikTok, Chinh Sach Quyen Rieng Tu va Chinh Sach Quyen Rieng Tu Cua TikTok
Shop cua Chung Toi, va cac Chinh Sach Ve TikTok Shop ap dung cua Chung Toi (nhu uoc quy
inh tai ieu Khoan 13, Quy Tac Su Dung TikTok Shop), ma cac ieu khoan cua cac chinh sach
noi tren uoc ket hop vao cac ieu Khoan nay bang cach dan chieu. Trong truong hop co bat
ky mau thuan nao giua cac quy inh cua cac ieu Khoan nay va cac Chinh Sach Ve TikTok
Shop, thi cac Chinh Sach Ve TikTok Shop se uoc uu tien ap dung, tru khi ieu khoan co
lien quan cua cac ieu Khoan nay ghi nhan ro rang rang ieu khoan co lien quan o uoc uu
tien ap dung, va, trong pham vi co bat ky mau thuan nao giua ieu Khoan Dich Vu Cua
TikTok va ieu Khoan nay, thi ieu Khoan Dich Vu Cua TikTok se uoc uu tien ap dung.
Neu Ban ang truy cap hoac su dung TikTok Shop thay mat cho mot doanh nghiep hoac mot
thuc the, thi (a) thuat ngu Ban va cua Ban bao gom ca Ban va doanh nghiep hoac thuc the
o, (b) Ban tuyen bo, am bao va cam ket rang Ban la nguoi ai dien uoc uy quyen cua doanh
nghiep hoac thuc the o co tham quyen rang buoc thuc the o voi cac ieu Khoan nay, va rang
Ban ong y voi cac ieu Khoan nay thay mat cho thuc the o, va (c) doanh nghiep hoac thuc
the cua Ban phai chiu trach nhiem ve mat phap ly va tai chinh oi voi viec ban truy cap
hoac su dung TikTok Shop cung nhu oi voi viec truy cap hoac su dung tai khoan cua Ban
boi nhung chu the khac co lien ket voi thuc the cua Ban, bao gom bat ky nguoi lao ong,
ai ly hoac nha thau nao.
Neu Ban khong ong y voi cac ieu Khoan nay, Ban khong uoc truy cap hoac su dung TikTok
Shop.
3. ieu Khoan Bo Sung Theo Khu Vuc Phap Ly
Neu Ban truy cap hoac su dung TikTok Shop tu mot khu vuc phap ly ma co cac ieu khoan bo
sung danh rieng cho khu vuc phap ly o, bang cac ieu Khoan nay, Ban cung ong y voi cac
ieu khoan bo sung ap dung oi voi Ban tai phan ieu Khoan Bo Sung Theo Khu Vuc Phap Ly .
Trong truong hop co su mau thuan giua cac quy inh cua phan ieu Khoan Bo Sung Theo Khu
Vuc Phap Ly ma lien quan en khu vuc phap ly cua Ban va phan con lai cua cac ieu Khoan
nay, thi phan ieu Khoan Bo Sung Theo Khu Vuc Phap Ly cua khu vuc phap ly tuong ung se
uoc uu tien ap dung va kiem soat.

5. Ve TikTok Shop
TikTok Shop La Gi?
TikTok Shop la mot nen tang thuong mai ien tu xa hoi ma chu yeu cho phep Nguoi Ban Chao
Ban San Pham en Nguoi Dung cua Nen Tang. Nguoi Dung mua San Pham tu Nguoi Ban uoc goi la
Nguoi Mua trong cac ieu Khoan nay.
TikTok Shop cho phep Nguoi Ban:
 truy cap vao trung tam danh cho nguoi ban ( Trung Tam Danh Cho Nguoi Ban ) va su dung
cac dich vu/tinh nang ma Chung Toi cung cap;
 tai len va quan ly thong tin San Pham va niem yet e hien thi en Nguoi Dung;
 tiep nhan on at hang va hoan tat viec ban hang cho Nguoi Mua;
 theo doi va quan ly on at hang va viec giao nhan;
 cung cap dich vu cham soc khach hang cho Nguoi Mua;
 thiet lap ke hoach quang ba, vi du nhu thong qua tinh nang lien ket; va
 su dung cac chuc nang va tinh nang khac (vi du nhu Trung Bay) ma TikTok Shop cung cap
cho Nguoi Ban.
Thong tin chi tiet ve cac tinh nang cua TikTok Shop co the uoc tim thay tai Seller
University ( Singapore , Indonesia , Malaysia , Phillipines , Thai Lan , Vuong Quoc Anh
, Viet Nam ).

Ban o, theo quy inh tai ia iem cua ca Nguoi Ban va cua Ban (goi chung la  Luat Ap Dung
). Ban cung phai tuan thu hop ong giua ban va Nguoi Mua oi voi viec ban San Pham ( ieu
Khoan Ban Hang )  xem tai ay. Viec Ban khong tuan thu Luat Ap Dung hoac ieu Khoan Ban
Hang se cau thanh vi pham co ban oi voi cac ieu Khoan nay.

Shop thay mat cho Ban.

Neu Ban uy quyen cho mot ben thu ba thuc hien cong viec neu tren cho Ban, Ban van se

6. Cach Su Dung TikTok Shop
Cach ang ky e tro thanh Nguoi Ban tren TikTok Shop

Truoc khi ang ky e tro thanh Nguoi Ban tren TikTok Shop, Ban se can phai co mot tai
khoan tren Nen Tang ( Tai Khoan ). Ban se uoc yeu cau cung cap mot so thong tin nhat inh
( Thong Tin Tiep Nhan ) cho TikTok trong qua trinh ang ky. Chung toi se su dung Thong
Tin Tiep Nhan cua Ban e anh gia xem Ban co uoc phep su dung TikTok Shop hay khong. Neu
Ban uoc phep, Ban se co the truy cap chuc nang Nguoi Ban thong qua TikTok Shop. Cac
huong dan ang ky e tro thanh Nguoi Ban va cac Thong Tin Tiep Nhan Ban can cung cap co
tai Huong Dan ang Ky Danh Cho Nguoi Ban Tren TikTok Shop ( Singapore , Indonesia ,
Malaysia , Philippines, Thai Lan , Vuong Quoc Anh , Viet Nam ). Viec Ban khong cung cap,
va khong luon duy tri Thong Tin Tiep Nhan hoan thien va chinh xac uoc xem la vi pham co
ban oi voi cac ieu Khoan nay.
Nhu mot phan cua qua trinh ang ky, neu Ban la Nguoi Ban tai Trung Quoc (bao gom ca Hong
Kong), Ban cung uoc yeu cau cung cap mot khoan at coc cho Chung Toi. Chung Toi (hoac mot
trong nhung ben lien ket cua Chung Toi) se giu khoan at coc trong thoi gian Ban tham gia
voi vai tro Nguoi Ban tren TikTok Shop. Khi uoc yeu cau mot cach hop li, Chung Toi co
the su dung khoan at coc e bao ve TikTok, Nguoi Mua va Nguoi Dung trong truong hop Ban
khong tuan thu ieu Khoan nay, phap luat ap dung, quy inh hoac huong dan, va/hoac bat ki
chinh sach, quy tac hoac yeu cau ap dung nao khac, bao gom nhung khong gioi han o viec e
boi thuong cho TikTok khi TikTok a thuc hien thanh toan cho Nguoi Mua do hanh ong hoac
San Pham cua Ban. So tien cua khoan at coc phai tra co the phu thuoc vao loai hinh cua
nhung san pham ma Ban du inh chao ban tren TikTok Shop. Neu khoan at coc cua Ban bi khau
tru, Ban se uoc yeu cau phai nop them mot khoan tien khac e bu vao khoan khau tru o va
am bao rang so tien at coc uoc giu boi Chung Toi la luon ay u. So du con lai cua khoan
at coc cua Ban se uoc tra lai cho Ban khi Ban khong con la Nguoi Ban tren TikTok Shop.
Ban phai chiu trach nhiem hoan toan oi voi bat ky phi ton, phi dich vu va chi phi nao
khac (bao gom nhung khong gioi han o cac hao hut do chuyen oi tien te) phat sinh trong
qua trinh chuyen khoan at coc cua Ban cho Chung Toi va khi Chung Toi tra lai so du con
lai cua khoan at coc cua Ban cho Ban. e biet ay u chi tiet chi tiet ve quy tac at coc,
vui long xem Chinh Sach at Coc Cua Cua Hang Xuyen Bien Gioi Tren TikTok Shop .

Cach Niem Yet San Pham
Khi Ban a ang ky thanh cong e tro thanh Nguoi Ban tren TikTok Shop, Ban se co the tai
len thong tin chi tiet ve (cac) San Pham cua Ban va niem yet chung e hien thi en Nguoi
Dung. Cac huong dan ve cach thuc hien viec nay va cac thu tuc kiem tra Chung Toi thuc
hien truoc khi hien thi ( Tieu Chi San Pham u ieu Kien ) co tai Seller University (
Singapore , Indonesia , Malaysia , Philippines , Thai Lan , Vuong Quoc Anh , Viet Nam )
va Huong Dan Niem Yet San Pham Tren TikTok Shop Cho Nguoi Ban ( Singapore , Indonesia ,
Malaysia , Philippines , Thai Lan, Vuong Quoc Anh, Viet Nam ). Viec Ban khong cung cap
thong tin chi tiet hoan thien va chinh xac ve San Pham cua Ban cho Nguoi Mua truoc va
sau Giao Dich Mua Ban se uoc xem la vi pham co ban cac ieu Khoan nay.
Viec niem yet San Pham cua Ban phai hien thi tat ca cac ieu khoan va ieu kien can thiet
theo luat inh (theo Phap Luat Ap Dung) cho Nguoi Mua truoc khi giao ket bat ky Giao Dich
Mua Ban nao. Viec ban khong cung cap cho Nguoi Mua cac ieu khoan va ieu kien nhu vay
truoc khi giao ket bat ky Giao Dich Mua Ban nao se uoc xem la vi pham co ban cac ieu
Khoan nay.
