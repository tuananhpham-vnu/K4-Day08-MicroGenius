# Seller Listing Regulations for E-commerce Support

This sample regulation describes seller responsibilities when creating product listings on an e-commerce marketplace. Sellers must publish accurate product titles, descriptions, photos, prices, inventory status, warranty information, shipping details, and category attributes. Sellers must not list prohibited goods, counterfeit goods, unsafe products, misleading claims, illegal services, or items that violate marketplace rules.

The marketplace may remove listings, restrict seller accounts, lower product visibility, cancel orders, or require corrective action when a seller violates listing regulations. Sellers should keep invoices, authorization letters, product certificates, and fulfillment evidence when requested by the platform. Product descriptions should match the actual item delivered to the buyer.

Listing quality affects buyer trust and customer support outcomes. Clear titles, complete specifications, and accurate photos reduce return and refund disputes. Sellers should update listings when product versions, stock, delivery promise, or warranty terms change. Repeated listing violations may lead to account suspension.
